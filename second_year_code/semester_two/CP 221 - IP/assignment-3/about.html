About The Platform

Why this system exists.

The platform collects simple health information and presents it in a clear format for community members and local health workers.

Purpose

CHIS supports awareness, prevention, and access to local services by organizing trusted health information in one place.

Users

It is designed for families, community officers, and health volunteers who need quick and simple guidance.

What It Covers

Core information areas

Preventive Care

Care reminders and scheduling for routine checkups.

Maternal & Child

Health notices for mothers and young children.

Nutrition

Hygiene education and balanced diet guidance.

Summary

The system improves communication between the clinic and the community through simple digital access.
